Securing Your AI Agent Fleet

Five Layers of Defense — From Identity to Audit

Every agent earns its access. Every action is verified. Every breach is contained in seconds.

The Problem

⚠
Over-privileged agents — Most AI agents run with the same credentials as the humans who launched them. One compromised agent = full lateral access.
🚫
Invisible agents — Today most AI agents have no identity, no enrollment, and no way to revoke access. If you can't see them, you can't manage them.
💣
Unlimited blast radius — A misbehaving agent can read, write, and delete across your entire estate before anyone notices.

Our Approach

🛡
Trust tiers — Four access levels: Blocked, Untrusted, Verified, Sovereign. Every agent starts low and earns its way up.
🔒
Defense in depth — Five enforcement layers, any of which can independently deny a request. No single point of failure.
⚡
Automated containment — Behavioral anomaly detection + circuit breaker. Seconds to contain, not hours.

Business Outcomes

✅
Zero lateral movement — Compromised agent can never escalate beyond its assigned tier ceiling.
📋
Audit-ready from day one — Every action logged in standard format. Full delegation chain traceable. Compliance-ready.
💰
Productivity preserved — Trusted agents get full access. Only unknown agents face restrictions. Security without slowdown.
